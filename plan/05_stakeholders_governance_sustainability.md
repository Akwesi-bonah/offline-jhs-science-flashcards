## Stakeholder map

Engagement methods are taken from the Engage2020 Action Catalogue.

| Group | Interests / values | Risks / burdens | Power | Engagement method (Engage2020) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JHS students** (main users) | Passing the BECE; understanding topics, not just memorising; free revision that works without internet | Wrong answers before exams; over-reliance or copying; English-only answers exclude some learners | Low: they use the tool but do not decide whether a school adopts it | **Focus group**: a small group from the pilot class tries the cards and says what was helpful, confusing or wrong |
| **Teachers** (JHS science) | Less preparation time; accurate, curriculum-matched content; keeping their professional role | Extra review work; being sidelined by the tool; blamed for errors they did not write | Medium: they review cards and control classroom use | **Scenario workshop**: teachers work through scenarios (wrong card, exam week, power cut) and set the rules for use; volunteers form the review panel |
| **Parents and community** | Affordable education; children's safety and screen time; local relevance | Device and power costs; worry about data and screen time | Low to medium: influence through the PTA | **World Café**: a discussion at a PTA meeting on benefits, worries and power or device costs |
| **School heads, district office, GES** | Curriculum fit; standards; exam integrity; cost | Reputational risk from wrong content; maintenance costs; unequal roll-out | High: they approve pilots and scaling | **Expert panel / consultation**: a short brief plus pilot results presented for approval before any scaling |

## How the groups connect to governance

- Students and parents are the most affected but have the least power, so the safeguards in
  `07_governance_brief.md` (no student data, teacher review, error reporting) protect them by
  default rather than relying on them to complain.
- Teachers hold day-to-day control: nothing reaches students without their approval.
- School heads and GES decide on scaling, so scaling only happens after a pilot and a review panel.
