## Important notes for anyone reusing this data

- **AI-assisted content.** A small number of cards are hand-written seeds. Most cards were
  generated with an AI assistant (Claude) and are marked in the `source` field as
  `"synthetic (Claude), needs teacher review"`.
- **Teacher review needed.** Synthetic cards have not all been reviewed by a qualified JHS
  science teacher. They may contain errors. Do not use them with students until a teacher has
  checked them.
- **No copied material.** No textbook, past-paper or website text was copied; answers are
  original wording of common scientific facts based on public curriculum topics.
- **No personal data.** The dataset contains no information about students, teachers or any
  other person.
